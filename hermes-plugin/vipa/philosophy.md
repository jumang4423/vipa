# vipa

**vipa** is *vipassanā*, cut short.

## The practice it is named after

Vipassanā is not a technique for feeling better. It is the practice of seeing what is
actually there — the sensation before the story about it — so that the story stops being
mistaken for the world.

The world that reaches a phone is almost entirely story. What a crowd finds interesting,
what a screen wants you to compare, what a number says about your standing: none of that is
sensation. All of it is a demand, arriving in the shape of information.

## What this builds

The phone is a KYF39: it can do very little and its battery lasts about ten days. Nothing on
it can ask to be looked at. One screen, four keys, and the thinking stays at home where you
can read it, edit it, and switch it off.

The machine at home has one job: **block the dynamics that collective fictions generate** —
comparison, rank, consensus pressure, the race for standing, the small hourly pulls that
exist only because everyone else is already in them. It does not argue with those dynamics
and it does not try to win against them. It keeps them off the phone.

What is left is what a life actually owes: **the minimum of your responsibilities in
society**. A deadline. A person waiting for an answer. A commitment you made. A change in
the world that alters today's plan. That, and nothing else.

## What it is for

- **real sensation** — instead of the story about it
- **plain ethics** — the kind that does not need a crowd to be right
- **peace, and stability** — the ground you stand on when nothing is pulling at you
- **a day you can account for** — without a scoreboard to measure it against

## What it is not

- not minimalism: the aim is not fewer things, but fewer questions
- not productivity: getting faster at the race is still the race
- not self-help: nothing here grows you
- not resistance: resistance stays in the same arena. The point is to leave it

## How it is decided

One question, and `criteria.md` is that question written out:

> Does this ask you to do something in the world today — or does it ask you to look at the
> phone?

If nothing is owed, nothing is sent. Silence is the default, and it costs you nothing.

## Honest state

The phone, the door, and the rules exist. Enforcement is still the agent's judgement, not a
mechanism: this repository has no classifier and no audit log yet.
